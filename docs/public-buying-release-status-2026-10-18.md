# Real-money buying release status — 18 October 2026

## What works

- An earlier owner-approved AAPL Stock Token purchase was confirmed and reconciled on Robinhood Chain. This proves that specific purchase, not every future wallet or account.
- The current owner wallet's permission has passed its onchain setup checks. Its next real purchase and a transfer out of that same wallet still need an owner-signed end-to-end check.
- The site prepares owner-approved transfers of USDG and approved Stock Tokens from either the current or earlier Steward wallet to its verified owner. It also prepares a pilot-only, owner-signed sale of the entire Stock Token balance back to USDG in that same wallet, with a live quote, fixed minimum payout, full Safe transaction simulation and fee estimate. A prepared sale still requires the owner's wallet signature and a successful chain receipt; no sale has been submitted by the agent.
- Markets, Alerts and Practice remain public. Real purchase preparation and new permission setup are restricted to the verified owner wallet listed in `STEWARD_LIVE_BUY_PILOT_WALLETS`. Missing or malformed configuration denies access. This is pilot admission, not a legal eligibility determination.

## What blocks a public buying announcement

1. **U.S. is excluded.** The issuer says Stock Tokens may not be offered, sold or delivered in the United States or to U.S. persons. An IP-country check alone cannot establish U.S.-person status.
2. **Nigeria needs an operating approval path.** The operator is in Nigeria, according to the founder. Nigeria is not named on the issuer's restricted-jurisdiction page, but this is not approval to offer these products there. The Nigerian SEC says only registered entities may promote investment services, advise or solicit funds from the public. Its ARIP checklist covers VASPs and digital investment platforms serving Nigerians. Obtain a written classification and registration, authorized-partner or exemption decision for Stock Steward's exact noncustodial purchase flow before inviting public Nigerian buyers. ARIP admission and its scope must be confirmed; an application alone is not approval.
3. **Other countries need separate review.** The issuer also names Canada, the UK and Switzerland as restricted, and lists Cuba, Belarus, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Ukraine, South Sudan, Sudan, Myanmar and Venezuela as prohibited-investor jurisdictions. The issuer says the list is non-exhaustive and can change. No country is automatically cleared merely because it is absent from that list. Each country added to real-money buying needs product-specific issuer terms, local platform analysis, a permitted operating entity or partner, verified customer eligibility, and a server-side admission decision. EU crypto-asset authorization alone may not cover tokenized debt securities: MiCA excludes crypto-assets that qualify as financial instruments.
4. **Current-wallet journey needs a funded owner test.** Verify one exact buy, receipt, visible balance, owner-approved transfer out, and an owner-approved sale where eligible. The prior AAPL balance has a quoted and simulated sell path, but no sale has been signed or confirmed. No agent should sign or submit the owner's wallet transaction.
5. **Broad release needs independent security review.** The pilot has internal checks and tight caps, but no independent review of wallet permissions and transfer/order paths has been recorded.

## Widest defensible public rollout

- Keep Markets, Alerts, Practice and educational pages public wherever the site may lawfully be accessed. These are separate from access to real-money order preparation and wallet permission setup.
- Keep real-money buying in the private pilot until the Nigerian operating pathway and the first destination-country pathway are confirmed. There is currently **no verified public real-money country allowlist**.
- Add countries one by one after written review. For each country, record the issuer's current restrictions and relevant Final Terms, Stock Steward's authority to facilitate purchases there, age/identity and residence/person-status checks, sanctions screening, ongoing location checks, consumer disclosures, and any permitted marketing limits. An IP address or wallet address alone does not establish eligibility.
- Deny U.S. location and U.S.-person status for these Robinhood Stock Tokens even if an onchain swap can technically settle. Use a different duly authorized product/partner for a future U.S. offering.
- Recheck issuer terms and regulatory permissions whenever the product, token, or operating model changes. Stop new order preparation if an approval, source, or eligibility decision expires or cannot be verified.

Do not describe the private pilot allowlist as proof of regulatory eligibility or as a public geofence. Keep a person-specific eligibility decision separate from wallet ownership before any public buying rollout. This document is a release-control record, not a legal opinion.
