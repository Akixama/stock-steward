# Real-money buying release status — 18 October 2026

## What works

- An earlier owner-approved AAPL Stock Token purchase was confirmed and reconciled on Robinhood Chain. This proves that specific purchase, not every future wallet or account.
- The current owner wallet's permission has passed its onchain setup checks. Its next real purchase and a transfer out of that same wallet still need an owner-signed end-to-end check.
- The site now prepares owner-approved transfers of USDG and approved Stock Tokens from either the current or earlier Steward wallet to its verified owner. A transfer is not a sale; the site has no real-money Sell button.
- Markets, Alerts and Practice remain public. Real purchase preparation and new permission setup are restricted to the verified owner wallet listed in `STEWARD_LIVE_BUY_PILOT_WALLETS`. Missing or malformed configuration denies access. This is pilot admission, not a legal eligibility determination.

## What blocks a public buying announcement

1. **U.S. is excluded.** The issuer says Stock Tokens may not be offered, sold or delivered in the United States or to U.S. persons. An IP-country check alone cannot establish U.S.-person status.
2. **Nigeria needs a legal operating-scope decision.** Nigeria is not named on the issuer's restricted-jurisdiction list, but Nigerian SEC rules and statements cover platforms facilitating digital-asset trading and foreign securities. Have Nigerian securities counsel or the SEC determine the registration, partnership or exemption path for this specific product before inviting public Nigerian buyers.
3. **Other countries need separate review.** Do not turn on a generic worldwide option. Each added country needs issuer/product restrictions, local platform rules, identity and location verification, and a server-side admission decision.
4. **Current-wallet journey needs a funded owner test.** Verify one exact buy, receipt, visible balance, owner-approved transfer out, and the available sale route where eligible. No agent should sign or submit the owner's wallet transaction.
5. **Broad release needs independent security review.** The pilot has internal checks and tight caps, but no independent review of wallet permissions and transfer/order paths has been recorded.

Do not describe the private pilot allowlist as proof of regulatory eligibility or as a public geofence. Keep a person-specific eligibility decision separate from wallet ownership before any public buying rollout.
